Identifying Silver: A Professional Guide

Premium Asset

Sterling Silver (.925)

Genuine sterling silver consists of 92.5% pure silver. It is an investment-grade precious metal that maintains intrinsic market value regardless of condition.

  • verified Hallmarks: Look for "925", "Sterling", or the British Lion Passant.
  • verified Natural Tarnish: Develops a warm, dark patina over time.
  • verified Resonant Sound: Produces a long, high-pitched ring when tapped.
Base Metal

Silver Plate

Silver plating involves a thin layer of silver electroplated over base metals like copper or brass. These items hold aesthetic value but limited metal melt value.

  • info Hallmarks: Often marked "EPNS", "Silver on Copper", or "Quadruple Plate".
  • info Base Metal Reveal: Often shows "bleeding" where brass or copper shows through.
  • info Dull Sound: Produces a short, flat "clunk" when tapped.
